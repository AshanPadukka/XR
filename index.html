Abstract

Research Abstract

This research provides the design, development, and performance assessment of a novel WebXR-based communication paradigm for a Distributed Industrial Teleoperation System. The system utilizes WebSocket full-duplex middleware for real-time VR-based industrial control within a closed-loop water circulation testbed.

The developed process has been validated at technology readiness levels of 3–4. Round-trip and end-to-end delay time was quantified with a mean of 85 ms (median 72 ms; std. dev. = 28 ms); and jitter measured at ±18 ms was determined at the network layer during normal operation using Wi-Fi 5/6. The immersive interface provided by the MetaQuest 2 headset, utilizing the WebXR Device API, enables the transmission of data from sensors at 10–20 Hz speed adequate for closed-loop monitoring and control within industrial environments.

Quantitative comparisons of the VR-based distributed industrial teleoperation system to traditional SCADA and physically controlled panel systems indicate that the VR-based system can complete tasks in nearly similar amounts of time to those two systems, but can improve spatial awareness and operator situational awareness.
